Why PACE

Choose motion evidence, not hidden attention.

PACE is less coupled to one model architecture or attention pattern.

01 / VLA-KIL limitation

Attention can become diffuse

With relative joint-angle actions, attention decay and its plateau can be less distinct. A flatter distribution weakens the boundary signal that VLA-KIL uses to decide where a prediction remains reliable.

02 / PACE advantage

Kinematics transfers better

PACE reads the predicted motion itself. It uses joint-speed valleys to locate motion-phase boundaries, without requiring internal attention tensors, so it is easier to reuse across VLA models, action representations, and network architectures.
