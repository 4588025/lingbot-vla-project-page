E1 / Relative action

Relative targets.
Time-aware normalization.

The arm predicts local joint displacement from the current observation instead of a global joint pose.

Action definition

Relative joint-angle

For every future position h, the 12 arm joints use Δq(h) = q(target,h) − q(now). The two grippers remain absolute targets.

−6.5%training loss
Δqh,j = qtarget,h,j − qnow,j
Joint-wise[12]

Pool all 50 future positions, then estimate one distribution for each arm joint.

Horizon × Joint[50, 12]

Estimate a separate distribution for every future position and arm joint.

Why we select Horizon × Joint

Early chunk steps contain small displacements, while later steps are typically larger. Separate statistics preserve this time-dependent scale instead of mixing it away. Tail padding is excluded, and inference uses the matching statistic for each horizon position.

Arm state remains [12]; gripper action/state are not split by horizon.
