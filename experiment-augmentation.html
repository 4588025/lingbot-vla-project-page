Implemented recipe

What the project actually applies.

A 300-step ramp transitions into four replayable branches. All randomness is tied to the global sample draw.

01 / Paired color

Photometric

Brightness 0.7–1.3, contrast 0.6–1.4, saturation 0.7–1.3; shared by views and current/future frames.

02 / Local variation

Gamma + noise

Gamma 0.8–1.2 or weak noise σ 0.003–0.01. Pixel noise is restricted to the primary student view.

03 / Primary camera

Light geometry

Scale 0.9–1.1 and translation below 6% per axis. No rotation, shear, or flip; wrist views keep their geometry.

04 / Sensor quality

JPEG + blur

JPEG quality 55–95, Gaussian blur, or 3×3 motion blur on visible primary student views.

05 / Foreground safe

Background erasure

A 5%–20% rectangle is clipped to safe background. Robot, gripper, interacted objects, and image borders are protected.

06 / View robustness

Primary-view mask

7% steady-state branch masks the main camera with raw255 midpoint 127.5 while preserving wrist cameras.
